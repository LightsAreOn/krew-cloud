Usage rules

Wordmark always left, sphere always right. Never reversed.

Clear space: minimum 1× cap-height of wordmark on all four sides.

Minimum size: 80px wide for full lockup. 24px for mark only.

Never skew, rotate, recolour, add shadow, or outline the logo.

Never place the dark variant on a dark background without sufficient contrast.

Never replace the sphere with a different graphic element. Same circle composition always.

05

Voice & Tone

Direct
No filler. No hedging. State the point immediately. If it needs a preamble, cut the preamble.
Warm operator
Professional and human. KREW is a premium product. Write like one — without being cold or corporate.
Confident
Don’t qualify every statement. KREW agents know their outputs. Write with conviction and specificity.
Precise
Use exact language. Avoid vague superlatives. If something is significant, say why and by how much.
Never use
Always do
